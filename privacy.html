Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Karakedi Shorts Factory is a desktop content publishing tool designed to help publish original educational technology videos to accounts owned and controlled by the user.

Information We Access

When a user connects a TikTok account, the application may receive authorization information and account permissions required to use TikTok's official APIs.

The application only requests access necessary for its content publishing functionality.

How Information Is Used

Information obtained through TikTok is used solely to authenticate the connected account and perform actions explicitly authorized by the user, such as publishing original video content.

Data Storage

Authentication credentials and access tokens used by the desktop application are stored locally on the user's device where applicable. They are not intentionally sold or shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

The application integrates with TikTok through TikTok's official developer APIs. Use of TikTok is also subject to TikTok's own terms and privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is only transmitted to third-party services when necessary to provide functionality requested and authorized by the user.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored authentication information may be removed by disconnecting the account or deleting the application's locally stored credentials. Users may revoke the application's TikTok access through their TikTok account settings where available.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and prevent unauthorized access. Users are responsible for protecting access to the device on which the desktop application is installed.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or legal requirements change.

Contact

For privacy-related questions regarding Karakedi Shorts Factory, please contact the application owner through the official project repository.
